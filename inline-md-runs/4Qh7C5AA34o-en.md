## The Importance of a Resume and How to Write It

Did you know that your resume only gets a quick glance from a hiring manager for about 7 to 10 seconds after you submit your application? That is if it’s even seen at all, as you have to pass through the AI filters when applying for these jobs. Let’s be honest, writing a resume for the first time or updating it after a long time can seem daunting and confusing, especially given its importance and the abundance of conflicting information. How many pages should it be? What type of template should you use? But as a hiring manager and a professional resume writer, I will simplify things for you. In this video, I will explain in detail how to write a resume from a hiring manager's perspective.

<!-- t:40 -->
## Understanding the Target Audience

I would like to start by emphasizing the necessity of knowing our target audience, and who will be reviewing the resume we submit. The hiring manager will be the first and most important, but we also need to consider the recruiter and others in the interview chain. Frankly, this is why most resumes fail. When I review resumes, I always look at them from the perspective: If I were the hiring manager for this position, how would I want this resume to look? There is a very specific process we use to analyze resumes. So, while we are preparing the resume, I will keep that process in mind.

<!-- t:99 -->
## Resume Formatting

When starting out, we don’t need any complicated templates or anything like that. We just need something very simple and basic. I’m looking for a single-column format. I’m not looking for anything with flashy colors, charts, or graphics, or anything that might distract from the primary task, which is to convince them that our resume contains the right qualifications for getting the first screening interview. I advise you to go to your preferred word processing program, browse resume templates, and choose a simple model.

<!-- t:135 -->
## Contact Information

Now, I’m in the 'Seer' program and I will add my contact information at the top. Clearly, we want hiring managers to be able to reach us. So, we need to ensure that we include our full name and geographical location at the very least, which is important. Even if you are working remotely, hiring managers still want to know the area you live in, due to time zone issues and so on. So, I want to add the location and phone number. Make sure to include your phone number and email address. I recommend adding a link to your LinkedIn profile if you have one.

<!-- t:190 -->
## Professional Profile and Competencies

The next section I usually add is the professional profile. The reason I include the professional profile is that I want the first thing the hiring manager sees when reviewing my resume to be how well I fit the job without any hassle. So, I want to clarify that I have the right range of experience, the correct job titles, and perhaps even experience in the required sectors. However, I want to point out something. I am not summarizing my career here. The reason is that I have a full resume to inform hiring teams about what I have accomplished in my career. So, I will make this section very focused.

<!-- t:276 -->
## Work Experience and Education

When writing this professional profile, I want to focus on brevity. I don’t want a huge block of text here. In my case, let’s say I’m applying for a job in technical recruiting and my level of expertise is, let’s say, proficient. I will add a few key responsibilities I have, along with some relevant sectors I have worked in. Now, the second section in the professional profile will be a list of competencies related to the job field I’m applying for. So, I will add a number of relevant competencies that I have previously identified and possess.

<!-- t:559 -->
## Adding Skills and Projects

Finally, we want to add skills. Skills will be slightly different from competencies. Skills usually relate more to software or specific methodologies you have experience with. Again, try to make them relevant to the job you are applying for. In this case, I have already uploaded a lot to my profile, but for you, take a look at what you actually have compared to some of the job postings you’ve applied for, and try to add as many relevant skills as possible. This will be important when the applicant tracking system analyzes and scans your resume.
